# Behavioral Questions

1. **Tell me about a time when you had to lead a team through a significant change.**
   - Situation: Our team was transitioning from waterfall to agile methodology
   - Task: Lead the team through this significant change while maintaining productivity
   - Action: 
     - Created a detailed transition plan with clear milestones
     - Provided training sessions for the team
     - Maintained regular communication through daily stand-ups
     - Addressed team concerns promptly
   - Result: Successful transition with improved team productivity and better project delivery

2. **Describe a time when you had to mentor a struggling team member.**
   - Situation: A junior developer was struggling with complex tasks
   - Task: Help them improve their skills and confidence
   - Action: 
     - Created a personalized learning plan
     - Provided regular code reviews and feedback
     - Paired them with a senior developer
     - Set clear goals and milestones
   - Result: Improved performance and increased confidence in their abilities
