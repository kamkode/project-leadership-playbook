# Conflict Resolution Questions

1. **How do you handle conflicts between team members?**
   - Answer: I approach conflicts systematically by:
     - Addressing them promptly
     - Encouraging open communication
     - Understanding all perspectives
     - Finding mutually beneficial solutions
     - Documenting the resolution

2. **Describe a time when you had to resolve a conflict between team members.**
   - Situation: Two team members disagreed on technical approach
   - Task: Resolve the conflict while maintaining team harmony
   - Action: 
     - Facilitated a discussion
     - Encouraged active listening
     - Helped find common ground
     - Created an action plan
   - Result: Team reached consensus and improved collaboration

3. **How do you handle conflicts between team members and stakeholders?**

     - Document the resolution

16. **How would you handle a situation where a team member disagrees with an architectural decision you've made?** [Intermediate]

     - Follow up to evaluate the decision's effectiveness
